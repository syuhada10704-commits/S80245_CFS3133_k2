About the club

We are a fan group for readers and viewers of the Harry Potter stories. We meet to discuss the books, rewatch the films and trade theories. For the official home of the series, visit Wizarding World. This is an unofficial student project.

Book and film nights

Each month we pick one story, read or watch it together, and compare what changed on screen.

House quizzes

Friendly competitions between Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw and Slytherin fans, with points for the winners.
