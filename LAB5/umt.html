About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as a Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) in 1979, providing facilities for practical training for students in fisheries and marine science programs.

In 1996, the centre was upgraded to a branch campus named Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, in 2001, it gained autonomy as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) before officially being renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007.

Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier marine-focused university, dedicated to research and education in marine science, aquatic resources, environmental sustainability, and maritime management.
